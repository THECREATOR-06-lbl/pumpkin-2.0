01 The Spot

A grill house built for slow evenings.

PUMPKINS.food sits in Bantama, in what regulars call "the lungs of Kumasi" — a warm, low-lit room of timber, brass accents, and an open charcoal grill that keeps the kitchen running past midnight.

"Amazing cocktails and smoothies, good food and positive vibes."

The kitchen leans into open-flame cooking — chicken and skewers finished over live coals — alongside a rotating cocktail and smoothie list. It's the kind of place built for a long dinner with friends rather than a quick bite, though takeout and delivery are both on the table.
